Somewhere between a garden and a good conversation.

Icaro trades the usual café buzz for something quieter — a serene, nature-filled room in Sopan Baug that leans modern without trying hard at it. Cosy, casual, unhurried. The kind of place people come back to just to sit for longer.

Indoors is soft-lit and calm; the outdoor section is breezy and green. Between the two, it draws a wide crowd — college groups, tourists passing through, and regulars who show up with a laptop and stay till the light changes.
